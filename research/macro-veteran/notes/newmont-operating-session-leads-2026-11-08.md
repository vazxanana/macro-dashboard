- The review flagged Red Chris 70% and Nevada Gold Mines 38.5% as proportionately consolidated interests; Merian 75% ownership with 100% site consolidation and NCI; Pueblo Viejo 40% and the Lundin Gold 32% interest as equity-method investments. Confirm the filed accounting language before applying ownership adjustments.
- By-product CAS and AISC use different other-metal-credit treatments. Holding other metals fixed while scaling by-product unit costs with gold volume can silently scale credits as well. The current workbook avoids that unsupported volume extrapolation.
- The reported gold-price bridge includes a $31m provisional MTM contribution, or $26/oz. Treating reported price as immediate physical cash settlement would miss pricing and collection timing.
