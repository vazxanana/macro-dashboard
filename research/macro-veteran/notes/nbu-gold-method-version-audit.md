# NBU gold accounting methodology: 2022–2024 legal-version audit

Checked 9 November 2026. This is a descriptive primary-source methodology audit, not a legal opinion or a price study. The preserved XAU numerical-response file was not opened. No gold valuation, numerical FX level, return, back-conversion or trading result is reported here.

**The 2021-labelled consolidation cannot be applied mechanically to all of 2022.** Amendment 127 was adopted 30 November 2021 and published 1 December, but commenced **4 January 2022**. Its attached changes move the BFIX observation from **15:30 to 15:00 Kyiv time** and the website publication deadline from **16:00 to 15:30**. The prior NBU 2020 consolidation was also retrieved and verified. These are legal calculation/publication clocks, not historical API receipt timestamps. [Amendment 127, PDF p 1 §4; pp 2–3 attached §§1(2),2(4)](https://bank.gov.ua/admin_uploads/law/30112021_127.pdf?v=19), [prior consolidation, PDF pp 1,7–8](https://bank.gov.ua/admin_uploads/law/Regulation_10122019_148_07102020.pdf).

The original 24 February Regulation 18 §13 only briefly states that the official rate is fixed; same-day amendment 21 explicitly names metals. Regulation 18's wartime precedence clause, introduced by 21, says other NBU acts apply insofar as they do not contradict it. The full-day freeze must therefore not be ignored merely because 148 remained in force. ## Formula, fallback, precision and distinct clocks

The preserved 2021 and captured 2024 consolidations identify these standing rules:

- **Gold source and official FX input:** III §8(1) combines the official UAH/USD input specified in III §7(1) with Bloomberg BFIX USD-to-metal quotations. §8(2) substitutes the latest available metals price from the official LBMA site or a trading-information system when BFIX is unpublished or unavailable because of operational risk. This is a conditional fallback, not proof of which source was used on an individual day. [2024 consolidation, PDF pp 6–7](https://bank.gov.ua/admin_uploads/law/Regulation_10122019_148_13092024.pdf?v=12).
- **Official USD is a separate constructed input:** III §7(1) refers to appendices 1–2. Appendix 1 uses a filtered transaction series and volume weighting; appendix 2 allows specified quote-based calculations and, if its alternatives fail, the official rate effective on the calculation day. The separate noon reference indicator is not interchangeable with the official USD rate used by §8. Wartime 18 overrides the input regime as mapped above. [2021 consolidation, PDF pp 4–5,11–14](https://bank.gov.ua/admin_uploads/law/Regulation_10122019_148_30112021.pdf?v=4).
- **Precision:** III §9 specifies two decimal places for metals accounting valuations; it does not, in the reviewed wording, specify a tie-breaking rule, truncation versus a particular rounding algorithm, or the complete intermediate arithmetic. No rounding method was invented. [Amendment 70, PDF p 1](https://bank.gov.ua/admin_uploads/law/01062020_70.pdf?v=19), effective 2 June 2020 from its publication/commencement clauses.
- **Effectiveness:** III §10 distinguishes calculation from next-business-day effectiveness and preserves the value already effective on the last business/pre-holiday day through intervening nonbusiness days. A value calculated on that final business day starts on the next business day. III §11 separately permits prior-business-day levels when working days are transferred. [2020 consolidation, PDF p 7](https://bank.gov.ua/admin_uploads/law/Regulation_10122019_148_07102020.pdf), [2024 consolidation, PDF pp 7–8](https://bank.gov.ua/admin_uploads/law/Regulation_10122019_148_13092024.pdf?v=12).
- **Publication:** the 15:30 post-127 website deadline is distinct from the 15:00 BFIX observation and next-business-day effectiveness. There is no fixed UTC or New York offset; the source states Kyiv local time. Removing the email channel on 1 November 2024 does not move the website deadline. [Amendment 127, attached §2(4), PDF p 3](https://bank.gov.ua/admin_uploads/law/30112021_127.pdf?v=19), [amendment 115, PDF p 1](https://bank.gov.ua/admin_uploads/law/13092024_115.pdf?v=8).

The NBU's 2024 consolidation lists 70(2020),140(2020),127(2021),115(2024). The 2025 consolidation adds 36(2025). This supports the captured formal 148 amendment chain and the absence of an annotated 2022/2023 amendment **to 148 itself**. It does not prove absence of overriding wartime, operational or calendar acts. The 2025 amended text was used only as an amendment-inventory cross-check; its revised clauses were not backported. [2024 inventory, PDF p 1](https://bank.gov.ua/admin_uploads/law/Regulation_10122019_148_13092024.pdf?v=12), [2025 inventory, PDF p 1](https://bank.gov.ua/admin_uploads/law/Regulation_10122019_148_24032025.pdf?v=13).

## Covered and still uncovered

**Covered:** the pre/post 4 January clock change; explicit wartime freeze/restoration;21 July full-day carryover;3 October removal of the fixed official USD input;1 November email-channel change; and source, fallback, precision and ordinary effectiveness clauses. Thirteen exact amendment/commencement pages were rendered and visually checked; PDF page numbers are one-based and may differ from printed attachment numbering.

**Uncovered:** the exact signing/publication hours on 24 February and 21 March; the first new calculation/publication/effective valuation after each legal transition; daily BFIX-versus-fallback usage and source-quote age; historical execution of the special-condition USD method; rounding ties/intermediate arithmetic; complete seasonal/working-calendar exceptions; and original historical file revisions/publication receipts. The parent calendar module handles seasonal exceptions separately. No date label, including a unique `calcdate`, is promoted to proof of a fresh gold quote. In particular, date labels can coexist with a documented freeze; no equality of numerical values was tested.

The parliament's 148/18 full-page capture attempts failed. They are recorded as failures, not silently replaced by third-party summaries. The decisive bodies here are NBU originals and dated/annotated consolidations. This remains a currently published producer representation, not original-byte authentication.
