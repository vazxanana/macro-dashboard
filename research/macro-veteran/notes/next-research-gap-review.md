# Next substantive research gap — bounded library review

Reviewed 8 November 2026. This is an assessment of the current local artifacts, not a review of all possible research sources or a claim about trading performance. No master deliverable or frozen V1 artifact was changed.

## Most important unproven capability

The library has not yet demonstrated a complete, real, point-in-time forecasting cycle: capture what was knowable, issue a numerical forecast before the result, preserve a fair baseline and registered case set, resolve from the declared primary source, then measure errors without selecting cases or versions after the outcome. That is the largest gap between the library's current capability and the user's ambition. More sources or more synthetic scorer tests cannot establish it.

This is predominantly a missing **demonstrated workflow and accumulated outcomes**, rather than a missing list of information providers. Genuine gaps in historical consensus captures and synchronized market quotes matter when attempting market-surprise and tradability claims. They do not prevent forecasting an observable macro release against a transparently constructed model baseline.

## Ranked next three deliverables

### 1. A real registered CPI nowcast cohort and its first completed scorecard

Use one narrowly defined target: first-release seasonally adjusted core CPI month-over-month, with a fixed issuance horizon. Register consecutive eligible cases before their outcomes and preserve all of them. Begin with the next officially confirmed release if sufficient time remains to capture the information honestly; do not backdate a forecast. Issue a point mean or fixed quantiles. Capture a contemporaneous model baseline such as the Cleveland Fed nowcast, or a past-only naive baseline, explicitly labeled as such. Do not call a model baseline survey consensus. Keep a missing consensus capture missing.

**Required primary evidence:** official BLS release calendar, first-release CPI tables and relevant methods/footnotes; raw dated source captures for every input; actual known availability/receipt evidence; contemporaneous Cleveland Fed snapshot if used; independently timestamped plan and issuance history; subsequently the actual BLS release artifact and outcome metadata. Use an actual receipt timestamp rather than treating the scheduled release time as proof of delivery.

**Completion would prove:** the system can carry a real case from evidence through an immutable forecast to honest resolution and loss comparison, including misses and missing baselines. The first case proves an operational analytical cycle. It does not prove superior forecasting skill; that needs a sufficiently large, comparable, prospectively accumulated cohort with dependence and uncertainty reported. The cohort should continue without choosing only favorable months.

### 2. A reproducible repeated-event CPI-to-gold reaction study

Freeze one instrument, release field, expectation source, event-window family and estimation design. Build a release-level table before fitting results. Separate macro surprise, rates/FX mechanism evidence and gold response. Use pre-event/placebo windows and document overlapping announcements. If exact historical consensus or intraday quotes cannot be obtained under valid access rights, produce a coverage and exclusion report and narrow the estimand honestly. Daily gold prices cannot substantiate a five-minute response.

**Required primary evidence:** archived first-release BLS values and publication records; genuine timestamped historical expectations from the originating survey/provider where available; licensed exchange/benchmark intraday quotes or a clearly labeled broker-demo feed with instrument terms, bid/ask, spread, source/receipt times and historical coverage; official calendars for overlapping releases. Training-only surprise scaling, fixed holdout and all searched variants must be preserved.

**Completion would prove:** the stated macro-to-market relationship was actually measured in a defined sample and tested against the predeclared comparison, with uncertainty and counterexamples. It would not by itself identify a causal effect, establish a deployable edge or transfer benchmark returns to cTrader execution. This is a demonstrated empirical workflow with specific data dependencies, not an invitation to add more registry entries.

### 3. A normalized gold-producer cash-flow and valuation sensitivity case

Extend one existing issuer case into a reconciled model for one company first. Bridge disclosed production/sales and realized pricing through royalties, costs, taxes, working capital and sustaining/growth investment to cash flow. Model gold/FX/energy sensitivities with explicit ownership and hedge treatment. Separate issuer guidance, analyst estimates and calculated values. Capture contemporaneous equity price, share count, debt/cash and expectations before interpreting valuation; vary assumptions rather than selecting one favorable terminal value. Add a peer only once denominators and accounting measures reconcile.

**Required primary evidence:** issuer filings and financial statements, non-GAAP reconciliations, mine-level operating disclosures and technical reports where relied upon, ownership/hedging disclosures, debt maturities and capital-spending guidance; timestamped relevant market/curve observations from originating venues or authorized providers. Unsupported sensitivities must remain labeled analyst estimates.

**Completion would prove:** macro and commodity scenarios have been translated into a transparent, auditable fundamental valuation model with a quantified range and identifiable failure conditions. It would not prove future share-price performance or justify a live trade. A subsequent forecast and resolution cycle would still be needed.

## Work-order implication

Start the real forecast cohort now, without waiting for another large source expansion. Use available work time for the repeated-event evidence table and one issuer model while the prospective labels mature. Keep cTrader observations demo-only. Prioritize observed failures, missing captures and model reconciliation over adding generic operating rules.
