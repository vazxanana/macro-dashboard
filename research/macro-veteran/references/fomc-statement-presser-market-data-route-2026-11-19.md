# FOMC stage research: public market-data route

The [San Francisco Fed U.S. Monetary Policy Event-Study Database](https://www.frbsf.org/research-and-insights/data-and-indicators/us-monetary-policy-event-study-database/) (USMPD; page dated 30 October 2026) is a high-value public source for N3. It separates **30-minute statement**, **70-minute press-conference**, **combined monetary-event**, and **30-minute minutes** windows, with futures, OIS, Treasury/TIPS, stock and USD-market changes. It also links a public workbook and surprise-code package. The [associated research paper](https://www.frbsf.org/wp-content/uploads/wp2025-30.pdf) reports that press conferences can convey independent policy-path news and that, after the 2022 tightening began, their surprises sometimes had the opposite sign from statement surprises. These are *market changes*, not the text of the decision or executable cTrader gold returns.

The current [24-meeting join scaffold](fomc-stage-join-gate-2022-2024.csv) uses the existing official [FOMC statement clock audit](../work/fomc-overlap-calendar-2022-2024.md): eight meetings per year, **12 with SEP materials**. The statement's dated 2 p.m. ET clock is converted to UTC and the SF Fed study's -10/+20-minute statement window is calculated. The [Federal Reserve meeting calendar](https://www.federalreserve.gov/monetarypolicy/fomccalendars.htm) lists press conferences for these meetings. Announced target-range bounds are extracted from all 24 archived Fed statements, with [source excerpts and SHA-256 hashes](fomc-target-range-extraction-evidence-2022-2024.json). The first meeting has no prior target range within this cohort; subsequent midpoint changes are **announced decisions, not market surprises**. The actual start and receipt time for each presser is **blank** pending event-level verification; do not infer it from a generic 2:30 p.m. practice. All market-surprise and gold-return cells are blank.

## Source acquisition route

- Official workbook: `https://www.frbsf.org/wp-content/uploads/USMPD.xlsx` (link from the SF Fed page; roughly 190 KB on the current page).
- Official surprise output/code: `https://www.frbsf.org/wp-content/uploads/monetary-policy-surprises.zip` (roughly 73 KB on the current page).
- Source paper defines statement window as 10 minutes before to 20 after the release, and press-conference window as 10 minutes before to 60 after the presser starts. It uses median prices in bracketing observation windows and notes exceptions for thin trading. Keep the publisher's window definition distinct from the next *executable* demo bid/ask used in a trading test.

An attempted direct workbook fetch from this sandbox on 19 November 2026 failed at DNS resolution (`No such host is known`), before any file was acquired. The source URL is verified by the public SF Fed page, but no workbook row or numeric surprise is asserted here. The next data pass should download the official workbook in an environment with access, record bytes/SHA-256 and receipt UTC, inspect sheet/column definitions and units, then join statement and presser records by meeting date. Keep the workbook vintage: derived principal-component surprises can be recomputed when the publisher updates its file. The [separate SF Fed monetary-policy-surprises series](https://www.frbsf.org/research-and-insights/data-and-indicators/monetary-policy-surprises/) measures a 30-minute rate response and offers both raw and orthogonalized variants; it must not be interchanged with an entire meeting-day return.

## Trade hypothesis and falsification

Predeclare three outcomes per meeting: statement-stage direction, press-conference-stage direction, and their combined effect. A fixed target-rate change can accompany a surprise in the **expected path**; use futures or yields to measure that path, not the announced target alone. For the demo XAU/USD test, separately measure: (a) whether a statement-stage rates/USD response is followed by gold continuation or reversal *before* the presser; (b) whether the presser adds an opposite rates/USD shock and reverses gold; (c) the complete combined window. The SF Fed's published shock is calculated from rate prices **after** an event; it can label historical outcomes but cannot be used as a pre-event input. For any post-event signal, enter only after the necessary quotes exist and use the next executable cTrader demo bid/ask. Compare with no trade and fixed one-stage momentum rules, including costs and skipped events.

Neither the USMPD nor the existing 24-meeting calendar includes gold bid/ask ticks. The publisher's finding about rates/USD does not demonstrate gold profitability or authorize live trading.
