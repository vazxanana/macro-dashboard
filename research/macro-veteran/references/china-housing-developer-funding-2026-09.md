# China housing activity and developer funding — September 2026

**Information date:** 19 October 2026  
**Observation period:** January–September 2026  
**Purpose:** A dated example of separating household housing transactions, construction pipeline, developer cash receipts and local-government land revenue.

## The official NBS snapshot

The National Bureau of Statistics (NBS) reported broad weakness in development and new-home sales, alongside a rise in urban second-hand home online-signed transaction area. Developer funds received fell faster than new-home sales, with a sharp fall in reported domestic loans.

| NBS measure | Jan–Sep 2026 level | Year-on-year change | What it measures |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Real-estate development investment | ¥53,364 亿元 (¥5.3364tn) | −20.6% | Cumulative investment by development enterprises; includes housing construction, land development, public-interest buildings and land purchases. |
| New starts | 340.31 million m² | −24.9% | Cumulative floor area of newly started buildings. |
| Completions | 232.71 million m² | −25.1% | Cumulative floor area completed to the applicable handover/acceptance standard. |
| New commercial-home contract sales area | 564.83 million m² | −13.2% | Cumulative floor area in signed new-home purchase contracts. |
| New commercial-home contract sales value | ¥53,799 亿元 (¥5.3799tn) | −13.9% | Total contract price for the same new-home sale universe; mix-sensitive, not a constant-quality price index. |
| Urban second-hand home online-signed transaction area | 617.46 million m² | +10.7% | Cumulative online-signed contract area (网签), reported from the Ministry of Housing and Urban-Rural Development (MOHURD); this is not a land-title registration count. |
| Completed unsold commercial-home area | 748.25 million m² at 30 Sep | −1.5% | Completed, available-to-sell-or-rent inventory at period end; excludes uncompleted presale stock. |
| Developer funds received | ¥56,813 亿元 (¥5.6813tn) | −21.6% | Cumulative cash actually received by developers for development. Domestic loans fell 33.1%; deposits/prepayments fell 14.8%; personal mortgage loans received by developers fell 22.1%. |

## Reading the combination

New-home contract area and value were falling, while urban second-hand online-signed area was rising. This is evidence of a shift in observed transaction mix and the uneven performance of primary and resale channels. It does **not** by itself establish that total housing demand increased: the second-hand measure has a separate source and coverage, and the NBS release does not provide one all-market transaction total on a perfectly matched basis.

Developer funds received fell 21.6%, faster than new-home contract sales area. The domestic-loan component fell 33.1%, while deposits/prepayments and developer-received personal mortgage funds also declined. These are cash receipts by property developers, not a complete measure of new household mortgage lending, bank property exposure or all refinancing. Read them as financing-channel components of developer liquidity.

Completed unsold inventory eased 1.5% year over year, but this is not total housing overhang: NBS defines it as completed area available for sale or rent, excluding uncompleted presold projects and specified non-commercial/use categories. A smaller completed-stock figure therefore cannot prove presale inventory or unfinished-project risk has cleared.

For prices, use NBS's separate [70-city new- and existing-home price indices](https://www.stats.gov.cn/sj/zxfbhjd/202610/t20261019_1965609.html), not sales value divided by area. The two indices use different methods: new-home prices use full coverage of local housing-administration online-signed data, while existing-home prices combine key and typical surveys, property-agency/platform data and field collection. In September, NBS tier-level commentary reported new-home prices up 0.1% month on month in tier-one cities, flat in tier-two cities and down 0.2% in tier-three cities; existing-home prices were up 0.2% in tier one and down 0.2% / 0.3% in tiers two / three. Since January 2026 the price series uses a 2025 comparison base with adjusted city weights, so preserve the methodology break in long comparisons. The city price series is not a direct nationwide transaction-weighted index. [NBS methodology and base note](https://www.stats.gov.cn/xxgk/sjfb/zxfb2020/202606/t20260616_1963946.html)

## How to build a reliable monthly series

1. Download and archive each original [NBS property release](https://www.stats.gov.cn/sj/zxfbhjd/202610/t20261019_1965615.html), its downloadable tables and publication timestamp.
2. Keep reported January-to-date totals separate from month-only flows. To derive an individual month, difference adjacent cumulative releases for comparable fields; retain vintage and note that NBS revises comparable growth calculations after statistical-system and inspection adjustments.
3. Keep new-home contract area/value, MOHURD-sourced second-hand online-signed area, completed inventory and developer funds in separate measures with their own definitions and coverage. Do not infer constant-quality prices from sales value divided by area.
4. Triangulate demand, completion and cash flow with the NBS 70-city price indices, household-credit data, developer filings and bond maturities. Use MOF land-transfer receipts as a separate local-government revenue channel, not a substitute for home sales or developer cash. For PBOC property-credit series and LGFV bond-refinancing monitoring, see the [China credit and LGFV route](advanced-market-intelligence-source-map.md#china-credit-property-loans-and-local-government-financing-vehicles).
