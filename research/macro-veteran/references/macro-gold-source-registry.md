# Macro and Gold Research Source Registry

**Purpose:** A working directory for a MeridianX research stack. Cadence is approximate; use each publisher's current release calendar before scheduling ingestion. Access terms apply to the exact use case, especially display, automated use, storage, and redistribution.

## Ingestion fields to retain

For each stored record, preserve: `source_id`, `series_id`, `observation_period`, `published_at_utc`, `retrieved_at_utc`, `value`, `unit`, `seasonal_adjustment`, `vintage`, `revision_status`, `source_url`, `license/use_class`, and `quality_note`.

For each event, preserve the pre-event consensus **as of its timestamp**, official actual, prior value as then known, revised prior value, and price response over a named window. If a historical consensus archive is unavailable, mark it unavailable rather than reconstructing it from current estimates.

- **Rates correction window:** New York Fed SOFR is published around 8:00 a.m. ET and can be corrected later the same day. Store the retrieval timestamp and, where event precision matters, the final same-day vintage: [SOFR publication details](https://www.newyorkfed.org/markets/reference-rates/sofr).
- **Gold reserve timing:** WGC monthly country data are reported with roughly a two-month lag and may omit unreported transactions: [method and update notes](https://www.gold.org/goldhub/data/gold-reserves-by-country).
- **TIC:** Release dates move with the publication calendar. Use the actual release archive rather than a fixed assumed lag: [TIC releases](https://home.treasury.gov/data/treasury-international-capital-tic-system).
- **October 2026 CPI year-over-year gap:** BLS says most October 2025 CPI indexes were not published and it will not publish October 2026 12-month changes for series whose October 2025 base index is missing. Prioritize official monthly changes and detail; distinguish official values from vendor/model reconstructions. The [October CPI pre-release plan](us-october-2026-cpi-pre-release-plan.md) records the event protocol and source-specific expectation snapshots. [BLS FAQ](https://www.bls.gov/cpi/additional-resources/2025-federal-government-shutdown-impact-cpi-faq.htm)

### High-frequency India liquidity and Brazil payment activity

**India RBI Weekly Statistical Supplement (WSS).** Use the [RBI statistics archive](https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/Statistics.aspx) to capture the WSS tables for liquidity operations—repo, reverse repo, variable-rate repo/reverse repo, MSF, SDF and outright OMO—alongside weekly RBI assets/liabilities, reserves, bank credit and deposits. The [6 November 2026 extract](https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/BS_ViewWssExtractdetails.aspx?id=63939) reports daily liquidity operations through 1 November, while bank business data are as of 15 October and reserves as of 30 October; the release labels relevant figures provisional. Preserve the reference date per table, not one date for the whole PDF. RBI changed bank-reporting fortnights from alternate Fridays to the 15th and month-end starting 15 December 2025, so avoid treating the resulting timing break as an economic step-change. Use the WSS with daily LAF/money-market releases to test whether INR funding operations, money-market rates, and currency pressure move together. Facility net flows are not a complete measure of market-wide liquidity, and reserves alone do not establish intervention.

**India e-way bill activity.** NIC describes a State/Zone dashboard showing e-way bills generated at five-minute intervals, and the GST Council's [GST System Statistics page](https://gstcouncil.gov.in/gst-system-statistics) links to e-way bill statistics. Archive dated snapshots only if current public access permits; a stable public history/API was not confirmed. For logged-in users, the [FAQ](https://docs.ewaybillgst.gov.in/html/faq.html) describes daily reports and recommends saving them each day, while the [NIC user manual](https://docs.ewaybillgst.gov.in/Documents/usermanual_ewb.pdf) also documents month-wise downloads; recheck current portal access and time limits. The [date-based API](https://docs.ewaybillgst.gov.in/apidocs/version1.03/get-eway-bills-by-date.html) requires registered credentials and GSTIN and returns only active bills for the requesting generator. Bill counts are document-generation activity for qualifying goods movements, not tonnes, declared value or completed deliveries; exemptions/thresholds, cancellations, Part-B updates and route/transport changes affect the count. Pair with GST collections, customs trade, rail/road freight and MOT port data; do not treat it as real goods volume or output.

**Brazil BCB Pix and SPI.** BCB's [Pix open-data catalogue](https://dadosabertos.bcb.gov.br/pt_BR/dataset/pix) provides monthly payment values/counts, with breakdowns by municipality, transaction nature, age, person type, region, initiation method and purpose; data are updated monthly from November 2020. The [Pix statistics page](https://www.bcb.gov.br/estabilidadefinanceira/pix-em-numeros-estatisticas) is dynamic; it showed observations through 31 October 2026 when checked on 8 November. Pix totals combine SPI-settled activity with participant-reported transactions settled on participants' own books. **India UPI complementary series.** The [NPCI UPI monthly statistics](https://www.npci.org.in/product/upi/product-statistics) provide transaction counts, values and participating-bank counts; the main page showed September 2026 at the time of review. Its [ecosystem statistics](https://www.npci.org.in/what-we-do/upi/upi-ecosystem-statistics) add P2P/P2M and category splits, but that page was much less current (latest split observed was August 2025); capture the reference month separately and do not use the split as a current read. The headline monthly series excludes same-account debit/credit transactions from August 2018. Use merchant payments as a channel-specific activity cross-check with GST collections, retail sales and RBI credit/deposit data; do not interpret all UPI growth as incremental consumption.

### Emerging-market FX: attribution and event-time protocol

Do not label a currency move “central-bank intervention” from reserve stocks alone. Use an evidence ladder and keep each source's measurement basis separate:

1. **Market reaction:** archive spot, forwards, swaps, local rates, basis and option volatility with exchange timezone and timestamp. This measures the market move, not its official cause.
2. **Instrument-specific operation record:** use auction notices/results or a central-bank intervention transaction archive where available. Record instrument, amount, settlement/maturity dates, gross versus net, and whether dollars were delivered to spot markets. Brazil's daily FX Lines with Repurchase stock is a direct temporary spot-dollar liquidity channel; BCB FX-swap notional is a synthetic hedge and should be modeled separately. Mexico's FX auction archive is event-based rather than a continuous intervention tape.
3. **Balance-sheet / reserve reconciliation:** compare reserve-stock change with disclosed flow factors, FX/gold valuation, income, debt transactions and other adjustments. Mexico's weekly reserve-account factors help explain weekly movement; Brazil's monthly intervention history and daily instrument stocks have different coverage and vintages. A reserve-stock change is still not itself an intervention flow.
4. **External-flow corroboration:** check balance-of-payments financial flows, banking FX settlement, external debt maturities and portfolio-flow sources at their own frequency. These are corroborating channels, not substitutes for transaction-level intervention data; residency-based securities flows do not reveal beneficial owner or derivative hedges.
5. **Inference label:** call an intervention event **confirmed** only when an official transaction/auction record identifies it; **corroborated** when instrument-specific stock or official flow decompositions align but timing or gross amount is unresolved; otherwise use **unattributed FX pressure**. Store source publication time separately from reference period, transaction date and settlement date.

For South African rate and funding analysis, retain JIBAR and ZARONIA as distinct benchmarks during the transition. SARB's 3 Nov 2026 revision supersedes older material that still shows the former 31 Dec 2026 cessation date; the newer page is the controlling source for the revised 31 May 2027 date. For auction-based sovereign-yield event studies, use announcement/auction time and contemporaneous when-issued pricing; do not use a later circulation/settlement date as if it were the information arrival time.

### Questions that often lead to overlooked releases

- **Is pressure on a currency domestic or external?** Check current account, financial account, international investment position, external debt maturity, FX reserve template, and banking-system FX settlement. A trade surplus alone cannot answer this.
- **Is official reserve movement valuation or intervention?** Compare reserve stocks with FX changes, gold valuation, balance-of-payments flows and the release notes. Do not attribute the change to intervention without a flow record.
- **Is weakness broad or concentrated in one sector?** Go from GDP to monthly sector data, then employment, new orders, inventories, credit and export volumes. Headline PMI, GDP or retail sales may conceal composition.
- **Is a global macro claim being made from the wrong vintage?** Compare the live harmonized series with the source-country release as published on that date. Current databases can silently contain revisions that were not known to investors at the time.
