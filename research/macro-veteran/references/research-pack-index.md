## What each guide contributes

### Evidence and research design

- [Research Analyst Operating Standard](research-analyst-operating-standard.md): source quality, time/vintage integrity, release surprises, event windows, and claims discipline.
- [Research Brief Template](research-brief-template.md): reusable format for a forecast or investigation, including counterevidence and resolution criteria.
- [Forecast Ledger Schema](forecast-ledger.schema.json) and [synthetic example](forecast-ledger-example.json): records immutable forecast snapshots, comparators, evidence links, resolution sources, score direction, target diagnostics, and scores.
- [Empirical Market Research Validation](empirical-market-research-validation.md): point-in-time backtests, benchmark comparisons, selection bias, transaction costs, and robustness checks.
- [Probability Forecast Calibration Playbook](probability-forecast-calibration-playbook.md): calibration, proper scoring rules, interval forecasts, and how to keep probabilities separate from conviction language.
- [Market Expectations Capture Playbook](market-expectations-capture-playbook.md): point-in-time Fed rate pricing, breakevens, surveys, and event-expectation capture without conflating market prices and objective probabilities.
- [Source Gap Audit Template](source-gap-audit-template.md): structured intake, overlap and independence check, official-source evidence matrix, vintage/access/revision controls, interpretation hazards and final registry/index audit for proposed datasets.
- [GDP revision and composition case](gdp-revision-and-composition-case-2023-q3.md): three archived releases, explicit missing GDI, 45 reported/calculated records, source hashes, revision chains and annualization arithmetic; no retrospective claim of a forecast track record.
- [Quality audit and assistant correction note](research-corrections-and-quality-audit-2026-11-08.md): material corrections, the V1 example replacement, and the distinction between schema checks and source verification.

### Macro, markets, and positioning

- [Macro and Gold Source Registry](macro-gold-source-registry.md): primary data access and source limitations across regions and asset classes, now spanning China, India, Japan, Thailand, Brazil, Mexico, South Africa, Saudi Arabia, the UAE, Australia, Norway, Switzerland and the euro area. ## The operating sequence

For a material claim, follow this chain:

1. **Define the question:** instrument, horizon, event window, and what decision the analysis informs.
2. **Find the closest record:** prefer the originating agency, exchange, regulator, company filing, or central bank over a republisher.
3. **Freeze what was knowable:** save release/publication time, retrieval time, vintage, definitions, units, and first print.
4. **Separate measurement from explanation:** say what was observed, what was calculated, and what is inferred. Name the main alternative explanation.
5. **Check dependence:** multiple pages can repeat the same upstream dataset. Count distinct measurement processes, not citations.
6. **State what would change the view:** define a future observation or disconfirming condition before the forecast resolves.
7. **Score the claim after resolution:** retain the original forecast and compare it with a declared benchmark using appropriate error or probability scores.

## Freshness and use notes

- Dated briefs, schedules, latest-release claims, vendor coverage, and source-table contents can become stale. Re-check the linked primary source and write down the retrieval time before making a present-day claim.
- An official dataset can still be lagged, revised, estimated, or incomplete. Availability does not establish that it measures the concept a market narrative claims.
- A broker demo quote or depth book describes that broker's feed and rules. It is not a consolidated benchmark-price or market-wide liquidity record.
- Public availability does not automatically permit automated collection, retention, display, or redistribution. Check the publisher's terms before building a feed.
