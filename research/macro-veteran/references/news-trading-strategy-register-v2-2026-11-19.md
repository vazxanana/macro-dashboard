# MeridianX news-trading hypothesis register V2

**Scope:** scheduled high-impact macro releases, with XAU/USD on the cTrader **demo** feed first. Other instruments are candidates only if the demo account actually offers them and their tick/contract terms are recorded. These are test specifications, **not proven profitable strategies or live orders**. A calendar's red-folder label supplies attention, not direction. The [36-month CPI/PPI→PCE test](cpi-ppi-to-pce-expanding-model-check-2022-2024.md) found no robust incremental signal from a broad PPI measure, so that result is not promoted into a trade.

## Ten distinct ways to test news

| ID / priority | Setup and connection | Observable rule to test on demo | Main data and failure gate |
|---|---|---|---|
| N1 / high | **CPI composition surprise → rates/USD → gold.** Separate headline, core, shelter and energy rather than treating the headline as the only surprise. | Compute each first-print surprise against a timestamped consensus. After the print, record whether available rate and USD-proxy quotes confirm a consistent response; test the next executable XAU/USD bid/ask for a fixed horizon. | [BLS CPI releases](https://www.bls.gov/bls/news-release/cpi.htm) and [component weights](https://www.bls.gov/cpi/tables/relative-importance/). Consensus, true receipt clock, intraday confirmation quotes and demo spread are missing. A daily Treasury close is not an intraday quote. |
| N2 / high | **Employment Situation multi-field surprise.** Payroll growth, prior-month revisions, unemployment, average earnings and hours can disagree. | Predefine a small vector of surprises rather than cherry-picking whichever field explains the move afterward. Compare gold response conditional on the first available rates/USD-proxy response. | [BLS Employment Situation schedule](https://www.bls.gov/schedule/news_release/empsit.htm), [release archive](https://www.bls.gov/bls/news-release/empsit.htm), and the new [36-release multi-field panel](employment-news-multifield-research-2022-2024.md). Household and establishment surveys measure different populations; timestamped consensus remains to be captured. |
| N3 / high | **FOMC stages:** policy statement, projections when published, then press conference. | Treat the 2 p.m. statement/SEP and later spoken communication as separate event windows. Test continuation or reversal from one stage to the next only after timestamped text/transcript and quotes are available. | [Fed meeting calendar](https://www.federalreserve.gov/monetarypolicy/fomccalendars.htm) distinguishes statement, projections, minutes and press conference; [SEP example](https://www.federalreserve.gov/newsevents/pressreleases/monetary20260916b.htm) is timed. Do not attribute a later gold move to the rate decision alone. |
| N4 / high | **Post-release continuation versus reversal.** A tradable hypothesis can exist even when the macro forecast has no edge. | For each release family, freeze a first observation window, entry lag, spread cap and exit horizon. Compare (a) same-direction continuation, (b) reversal after a measured overshoot, and (c) no trade. Use executable bid/ask and count skipped/rejected orders. | Requires synchronized cTrader demo ticks and actual release receipt. The first print can widen spreads and cause missed entries; bar-close midprices are insufficient. This is a market-response test, not an economic nowcast. |
| N5 / medium | **Claims, JOLTS and ECI → later labor/Fed information.** Use earlier observations to update a later-release forecast, then test whether the later surprise changes rates and gold. | At each payroll or FOMC cutoff, use only claims/JOLTS/ECI actually published by then. Score a baseline and an augmented forecast before any release-day trade test. | [DOL weekly claims](https://www.dol.gov/ui/data.pdf), [BLS JOLTS release schedule](https://www.bls.gov/schedule/news_release/jolts.htm), [JOLTS revision FAQ](https://www.bls.gov/jlt/jltfaq.htm), and [ECI schedule](https://www.bls.gov/schedule/news_release/eci.htm). Reference months differ; JOLTS is preliminary and revised. |
| N6 / medium | **CPI and PPI detail → PCE.** Forecast the later BEA original-release core PCE print, then separately test the PCE day reaction. | Replace the weak aggregate PPI feature with BEA-source-mapped component surprises; compare against CPI-only and prior-PCE baselines with expanding training and a prospective ledger. | [BEA detailed source-map explanation](https://www.bea.gov/help/faq/521) and the [36-month first-release panel](cpi-ppi-to-pce-36-month-source-and-baseline-2022-2024.md). Historical crosswalk, weights, adjustments and timestamped consensus remain open. |
| N7 / medium | **Retail sales + prices → real consumption / GDP.** Retail is nominal and goods-heavy; CPI/PCE prices help frame real demand. | Score whether first-print retail components add to a pre-BEA real PCE or GDP component forecast. If a surprise trades, use the *retail consensus* and immediate market response, not the subsequent BEA outcome. | [Census advance retail schedule](https://www.census.gov/retail/release_schedule.html), [historical releases](https://www.census.gov/retail/marts/historic_releases.html), and [BEA GDP vintage explanation](https://www.bea.gov/news/gdp-release-additional-information). Retail reports are revised and omit much service spending. |
| N8 / medium | **Treasury refunding and auction results → yield pressure → gold.** Supply announcements and auction outcomes are separate news. | Compare result yield to a timestamped when-issued market yield, not merely to the prior auction. Then test rates/USD response and executable gold return; separate tenor and auction type. | [Treasury announcement/results archive and query](https://home.treasury.gov/policy-issues/financing-the-government/treasury-marketable-and-non-marketable-securities) and [auction dataset](https://fiscaldata.treasury.gov/datasets/treasury-securities-auctions-data/). When-issued quote, allocation details and intraday yields are missing. Bid-to-cover alone is not a surprise measure. |
| N9 / exploratory | **ISM activity versus price components.** New orders/employment and prices can point in different growth/inflation directions. | Capture the entire released component vector and timestamped expectations. Test whether the rates/USD response distinguishes growth-led from inflation-led gold moves. | [ISM manufacturing release](https://www.ismworld.org/supply-management-news-and-reports/reports/ism-pmi-reports/pmi/september/) explains index components and seasonal-adjustment differences. Release licensing, historical first prints, consensus and demo quotes need review. |
| N10 / low for gold | **EIA petroleum inventories → oil → inflation expectations.** A direct oil trade may be more natural than a gold trade if the demo account has the relevant symbol. | Use crude, gasoline, distillate, refinery and import/export surprises together; check an executable oil quote first. Gold is only an indirect secondary hypothesis. | [EIA WPSR schedule](https://www.eia.gov/petroleum/supply/weekly/schedule.php) and [petroleum data](https://www.eia.gov/dnav/pet/pet_move_top.asp). Holiday shifts, revisions and multiple simultaneous oil fields complicate a one-number inventory rule. |

## A concrete first demo experiment: N1/N2 post-release confirmation

1. **Before release:** freeze the release family, reference month, announced clock, consensus snapshot, data-field hierarchy, excluded overlapping events, XAU/USD symbol, quote clock, maximum acceptable spread, observation lag and exit horizon. Preserve no-trade days.
2. **At first availability:** save the agency first print, actual receipt time and every simultaneous field/revision. Compute surprise only against the frozen consensus. A missing consensus makes this a *market-response* test, not a surprise-conditioned trade.
3. **After the first fixed observation window:** compare the available 2-year rate instrument and USD proxy with their own pre-release quotes. If intraday rates are unavailable, label that filter unavailable; the [Treasury public yield feed](https://home.treasury.gov/resource-center/data-chart-center/interest-rates/TextView?type=daily_treasury_yield_curve) is daily and cannot fill it. Test continuation and reversal as separately frozen candidates, then enter only at the **next executable demo bid/ask** after the decision time.
4. **At exit:** use the opposing executable side, include spread/slippage/latency and rejected orders, and score every eligible event against no trade and a simple release-window momentum rule. Report return distribution by event family and year; do not combine unlike events to manufacture a large sample.

## Practical order of work

First collect a **prospective** cTrader demo quote/clock log and timestamped consensus for CPI and Employment Situation, because these decide whether N1/N2/N4 can be evaluated as trades. The [expectation source/timing audit](news-expectation-source-and-timing-gates-2026-11-19.md) identifies candidate feeds and the pre-release snapshot gate; the 2022–2024 payroll revision fields are now captured in the [employment panel](employment-news-multifield-research-2022-2024.md). Build first-release revision fields for retail next. Then test FOMC stages and auction results, whose text/market clocks require more care. Keep PCE component nowcasting as a forecast project until its historical source map is versioned. One family passes to the next stage only if the forecast or executable-return comparison survives costs, temporal exclusions and a forward demo period.

**Boundary:** No signal in this register is validated for live trading. No live route should be enabled from this document.
