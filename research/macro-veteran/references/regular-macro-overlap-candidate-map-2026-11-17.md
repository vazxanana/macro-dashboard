# Regular macro releases matching frozen Treasury window dates

A mechanical join of the eight-source audit's **regular release dates** to the ending dates of the **133 existing Treasury study intervals** yields **55 event rows on 43 distinct dates**. Thirty-eight are DOL weekly initial claims; seven are BEA GDP; three each are BEA Personal Income and Outlays, Census advance retail sales and Federal Reserve industrial production; one is BLS Employment Situation. Ten dates have more than one listed release. The accompanying [date-match inventory](../work/regular-macro-calendar-candidate-date-matches-2026-11-17.json) binds source file hashes and each matched date.

This is a **review queue**, not an interval verdict. The join uses only matching calendar dates. A release on another date, a reissue, a date-only correction, or a supplementary revision could still matter inside an interval. The calendar inventories themselves also need correction and clock coverage review. Therefore the [frozen broader-exclusion rule](treasury-cpi-study-protocol-2022-2024.json) has not been applied, no window has been called clean, and no controls or yield results have changed.

**Later header check:** all **38 DOL date-match candidates** were fetched from their recorded official URLs. Every first-page header states 08:30 Eastern on the same calendar date; 37 are Thursdays and the 27 November 2024 release is a Wednesday holiday shift. The [DOL header report](../work/dol-claims-window-header-followup-2026-11-17.md) and [machine inventory](../work/dol-claims-window-header-followup-2026-11-17.json) preserve receipts, PDF byte hashes and UTC conversions. A separate file check passed all 38 PDF byte/size/hash bindings. These headers strengthen the schedule evidence for these candidates; correction and actual first-publication evidence remain open.

A [separate notice triage](../work/macro-notice-window-triage-2026-11-17.md) found **nine known extra/revision notice dates** that meet or precede one of nine interval ending dates. Several occur on the *preceding* calendar day, which confirms that the 55-row regular same-date queue is insufficient as a complete overlap filter. Their intraday clocks or family scope remain unresolved; the one G.17 annual revision with a stated noon clock is a source lead, not an applied exclusion.

The file selects date and stated clock metadata only. It does not inspect economic values or compute Treasury moves, forecast errors or returns. It narrows the next source review to the named dates while retaining the full 133-interval denominator and the open coverage gate.
