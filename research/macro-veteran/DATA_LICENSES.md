# Data, attribution and explicit exclusions

The user's confirmed scope is private research and trading. This package is intended for that handoff; it is not a grant to redistribute vendor databases or publish paid-data extracts.

- Authored research notes, schemas, scripts, calculations, models, audit records and factual financial-table extractions are included. Publisher ownership is not transferred.
- Official BLS releases, archive/calendar/API/feed captures and BEA GDP releases are included as US government statistical materials. Retain source URLs, receipts and hashes. Embedded publisher notices, seals and third-party material do not acquire a new blanket licence through this package.
- The Federal Reserve Bank of Cleveland monthly chart component explicitly declares [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Attribution: **Federal Reserve Bank of Cleveland, Inflation Nowcasting Monthly Month-Over-Month**, from the [official page](https://www.clevelandfed.org/indicators-and-data/inflation-nowcasting) and its [monthly JSON](https://www.clevelandfed.org/-/media/files/webcharts/inflationnowcasting/nowcast_month.json?sc_lang=en). The original JSON is unchanged. Derived coverage/cohort files and their extraction scripts are separately identified. Licence attributes and original page/data hashes are in `work/raw/cpi-expectations-history/chart-license-evidence.json`. The page/article licence is distinct and must not be substituted for the chart's data licence.
- Full Cleveland page/paper bodies, extracted full text and page images are excluded from this shareable batch; authored methodology reviews and capture manifests remain.
- GLD raw XLSX, bar-list PDF and full issuer/terms pages are excluded because the issuer's usage and redistribution restrictions require separate attention. Source routes, payload hashes, counts/schema profiles and authored findings remain. Private trading purpose alone does not establish permission for every derivative use. See [issuer terms](https://www.spdrgoldshares.com/terms-and-conditions/) and `work/gld-historical-data-route.md`.
- Full Newmont publisher HTML filings are excluded; relevant extracted factual numerical tables, source URLs/hashes, authored research and finished model remain. Obtain original filings directly from SEC links in source manifests if needed.
- Dependency trees, caches and external-runtime links are omitted. Duplicate expanded old packages are represented by the unchanged original ZIPs.

`batch-inventory.json` provides individual paths, sizes, SHA-256 hashes and reasons for raw-file omissions. A blank source URL there means to consult the included capture manifests or source notes; it does not assert that no original source exists. Excluded raw files remain in the original local workspace and were not deleted.

They are current agency source versions, not independently authenticated original historical wire bytes. The separate raw NBU price response is held out under the stated working data-quality boundary until methodology/vintage audit; this is not a finding that NBU prohibits use. Prior delivery ZIPs and dependencies are omitted as duplicate/runtime content; all current research documents and finished model files remain included. See the individual inventory records.
