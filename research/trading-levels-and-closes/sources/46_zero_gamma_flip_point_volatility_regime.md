## Reported content
> "The gamma flip (also called the zero-gamma level) is the underlying price at which aggregate dealer gamma exposure changes sign — from net positive (above the flip) to net negative (below the flip)... When the underlying trades above the gamma flip, the market is in a positive-gamma regime, expect low volatility, mean reversion, and price pinning. When the underlying trades below the gamma flip, the market enters negative gamma, expect higher volatility, trending moves, and the potential for large selloffs." (paraphrased/aggregated from vendor sources)
