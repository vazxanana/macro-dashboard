## Relevance
CTA trigger levels are a real and market-relevant phenomenon (systematic trend-following flows can create reflexive breakout/breakdown acceleration), but the actual numeric levels are **not publicly, freely, or reliably available** — they are proprietary bank-desk or vendor-model estimates, not a clean data feed a dashboard could ingest on a schedule. This should be flagged as a qualitative risk factor (momentum/feedback-loop risk near round-number/technical breaks) rather than a quantifiable daily input; do not fabricate specific "CTA trigger price" numbers.
