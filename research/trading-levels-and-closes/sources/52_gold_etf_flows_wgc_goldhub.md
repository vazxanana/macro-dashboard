## Relevance
Of all the flow-based signals surveyed, ETF holdings/flows have the **shortest lag** (days, via weekly update) and thus are the most plausible candidate for a near-daily-cadence macro input, though a true daily gold ETF flow figure is not confirmed — the finest granularity found is weekly (Monday-published). Monthly narrative reports add color but are not needed for a numeric flow input. Should be logged as "weekly, ~2-7 day lag" in any information-source table, not daily.
