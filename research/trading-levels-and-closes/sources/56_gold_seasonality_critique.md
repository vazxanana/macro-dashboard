## Relevance
Directly supplies the skeptical counter-view requested: even seasonality-friendly industry sources concede the effect is secondary to macro/liquidity drivers and that reliability decays through the year. Notably, this source's claim that reliability is "lowest in November" directly conflicts with source 55's claim that November is one of only two statistically significant months — a clear signal that gold seasonality claims are inconsistent across popular sources and likely partly data-mined/cherry-picked depending on the sample window and methodology used by each writer. **Overall honest verdict: weak-to-moderate seasonal tendency exists (autumn strength is the most repeated claim across independent sources) but specifics vary by source, sample period, and methodology, and no source found rises above blog/single-study level rigor — treat as a low-weight, low-confidence input, not a tradeable edge on its own.**
