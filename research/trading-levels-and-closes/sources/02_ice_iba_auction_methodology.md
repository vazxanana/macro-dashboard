## Reported content
> "IBA's methodology underpinning the LBMA Gold Price is an electronic auction run twice daily at 10:30am and 3:00pm London time. The auction process provides a market-based platform for buyers and sellers to trade physical spot gold." (paraphrased from search summary)
> "The modern auction features up to 15 direct participants submitting anonymous net buy or sell quantities in discrete rounds, with the chair adjusting the opening price iteratively until residual imbalances approach zero. If the difference between the buying and selling interest at the completion of a Round is more than the 'Imbalance' threshold (currently set at 10,000 troy ounces), the auction moves to a further Round." (reported figure — not verified against primary text)
> Governance: "IBA does this through its LBMA Gold and Silver Auction Rule Book that respective Auction Participants are required to adhere to and through the Precious Metals Oversight Committee." (paraphrased)

## Relevance
Details the mechanics of price formation at the two benchmark times: an iterative Walrasian-style auction among up to 15 banks/participants that only clears when net imbalance falls under a 10,000 oz threshold. This means the AM/PM fix levels are not a single trade print but a converged clearing price for large net order flow — relevant to understanding why price can gravitate toward/away from round levels during the auction window.
