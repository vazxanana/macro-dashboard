## Relevance
Central to question 4's gold-specific caveat: COMEX gold futures do have a formally defined, exchange-administered settlement window (13:29-13:30 ET, a ~1-minute VWAP-based calculation) used for margin and mark-to-market — this is a genuine, mechanically privileged price, analogous in function (though not method) to an equity closing auction. However, this COMEX futures settlement time (~1:30pm ET) is DIFFERENT from the informal "5pm New York" daily-bar convention used by most retail spot-gold/FX charting platforms, and different again from the LBMA's twice-daily London auctions (10:30/15:00 London). This multiplicity of "closes" for gold is itself evidence against a single privileged daily close for the spot/OTC gold market.
