## Relevance
Establishes the two exact daily clock times (10:30 London / "AM") and (15:00 London / "PM") at which institutional gold price-setting auctions occur. These are the two moments each trading day when large real-money and hedging flow is contractually concentrated — i.e., the "tradeable levels" institutions are forced to transact at or reference, independent of any technical chart level.
