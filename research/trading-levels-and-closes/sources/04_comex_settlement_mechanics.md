## Reported content
> "The settlement period is defined as 13:29:00 to 13:30:00 ET for the Active Month" — i.e., approximately 1:29-1:30 PM Eastern Time (roughly 6:29-6:30 PM UK time). (reported figure — not verified against primary text)
> "COMEX Gold futures (GC) are settled by CME Group staff based on trading activity on CME Globex during the settlement period." (paraphrased)
> Tiered fallback method: "the settlement price is determined to form an implied market in the contract to be settled, using implied markets along with the outright bid/ask market to derive the best possible bid and ask... In the absence of relevant CME Globex data, the [Global Command Center] may consider block trade information for settlement derivation, with all block trade information required to be emailed to COMEXSETTLES@cmegroup.com before the end of the settlement window." (reported figure — not verified against primary text)

## Relevance
Identifies the exact 60-second COMEX settlement window (13:29:00-13:30:00 ET) used to mark futures settlement prices — the number used for margin calls, daily P&L marking, and many index/contract references tied to "COMEX gold settlement." This is a third distinct institutionally-privileged clock time (in addition to the two London fixes) and clarifies it is a volume-weighted/derived Globex-activity price, not a single trade or auction.
