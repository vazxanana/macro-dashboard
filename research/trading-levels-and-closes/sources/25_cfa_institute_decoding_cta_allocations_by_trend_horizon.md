## Relevance
Directly relevant to Question 4 (do CTAs actually rebalance on weekly signals). Confirms that CTA industry practice spans a *range* of trend horizons (measured in trading days, e.g. 20/60/125/250/500-day lookbacks) rather than a single canonical "weekly close" convention — meaning the "weekly bar" is one arbitrary point among many horizons funds actually run, not a uniquely privileged one. This cuts against a strong version of "the weekly close is special" and supports the more modest claim that *some* CTA capital operates on ~1-month (~20-day) horizons that loosely track weekly-ish signal changes, alongside much longer/shorter horizons.
