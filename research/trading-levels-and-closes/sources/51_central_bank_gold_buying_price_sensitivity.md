## Relevance
Directly answers the central question: search evidence points to a **mixed verdict** — central bank buying is largely **programmatic/mandate-driven** (reserve diversification, de-dollarization, sanctions-risk hedging) rather than tactically opportunistic, but multiple sources independently claim a secondary "buy-the-dip, don't sell-the-rip" asymmetry. This is consistent with theory (official reserve managers are not P&L-driven short-term traders) but the "accelerate on dips" claim is asserted without a rigorous quantitative event study in any source found — treat as a plausible-but-unverified directional bias, not a hard floor. Given the ~2-month IMF reporting lag, this cannot function as a timely daily/weekly trading signal regardless of its truth.
