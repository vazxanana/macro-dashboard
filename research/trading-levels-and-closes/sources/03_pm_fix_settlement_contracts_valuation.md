## Reported content
> "The PM fix is a benchmark gold price established twice each trading day through the LBMA auction process. The PM fix is set at 3:00 PM London time and is by far the more commonly used, serving as the reference for the majority of delivery contracts, ETFs, and settlements." (paraphrased)
> "The LBMA Gold Price is the formal benchmark used across the global gold market for settlement, accounting, fund valuation, and physical delivery contracts. Banks, refineries, mining companies, and central banks use the LBMA fix for the settlement of delivery contracts, the accounting of precious metal holdings, and the calculation of ETF share values." (paraphrased)

## Relevance
Establishes that among the two daily auctions, the 15:00 London ("PM") fix is the more heavily referenced one for real-world contractual settlement — mining company hedges, refinery contracts, and ETF share valuation disproportionately point to the PM print rather than the AM print. This elevates 15:00 London time specifically as an institutionally "privileged" moment beyond just being a benchmark auction.
