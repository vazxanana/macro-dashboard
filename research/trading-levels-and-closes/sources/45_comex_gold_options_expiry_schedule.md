## Relevance
Directly answers question 4: COMEX gold options (GC) now expire on a near-daily cadence during the business week (Mon/Tue/Wed/Thu/Fri weeklies added progressively 2023-2024) in addition to the standard monthly options expiry. This is a much denser schedule than equity index options (which cluster on Fridays/monthly OPEX). Practically, this means "options expiry as event risk" is nearly a daily phenomenon for gold rather than a rare, high-signal date — reducing the value of flagging expiry dates as a distinct, differentiated risk marker unless the monthly/quarterly expiries (which concentrate the most OI) are specifically distinguished from the low-OI daily weeklies.
