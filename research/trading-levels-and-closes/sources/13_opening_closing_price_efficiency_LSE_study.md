## Reported content
> "Research analyzing 73.62 million London Stock Exchange trades found that while opening auctions provide highly efficient prices for large volume stocks, closing prices only lose small fractions of informational efficiency achieved during normal trading hours." (paraphrased from search summary)

## Relevance
Bears on central question 3: this study suggests closing prices set via call auction are close to (but not necessarily superior to) the informational efficiency built up during the continuous trading session — i.e., the auction mostly "locks in" efficiency already achieved intraday rather than adding new information on top of it. This is a more moderate finding than either "close is uniquely privileged" or "close adds nothing," and should be read alongside the more skeptical Bogousslavsky & Muravyev result (file 11).
