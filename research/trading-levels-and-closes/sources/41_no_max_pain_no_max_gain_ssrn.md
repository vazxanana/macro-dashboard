## Relevance
This is the one academic-grade paper found that directly evaluates Max Pain theory empirically over a long sample. Reported finding: weak-to-moderate predictive power concentrated in small-cap/illiquid names, much weaker for large, liquid, well-arbitraged names — which is the opposite of where gold (a massive, deep, professionally-arbitraged futures/options market) would sit. This is a meaningful caveat against extrapolating Max Pain's modest equity effect to gold.
