## Relevance
PDH/PDL is one of the most widely used reference levels in retail intraday trading (this dashboard's likely candidate for "locked" reference levels), but **no rigorous academic study analogous to Osler's bootstrap-vs-random-levels methodology was found for PDH/PDL specifically**. The evidence base here is folklore-adjacent: single-stock/single-instrument retail statistics (e.g., "71% of the time..."), self-reported and unaudited, from broker/education sites with a commercial incentive to make setups sound edge-having. The qualitative logic (a level that many participants are watching — because it's an obvious, cheaply computed reference point — plausibly gets defended/reacted to more than an arbitrary price) is consistent with the self-fulfilling-prophecy mechanism, but this specific level type has weaker/no academic backing found — it should be graded lower than round numbers (Osler) or published S/R levels (Osler), and higher only than pure astrology-tier TA claims.
