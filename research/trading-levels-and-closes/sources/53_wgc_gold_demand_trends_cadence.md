## Relevance
Confirms GDT is fundamentally unsuitable as a daily or even weekly trading input — it is a **quarterly, ~1-month-lagged** structural/demand report best used for slow macro-narrative calibration (e.g., "is central bank buying decelerating year-over-year") rather than for timing entries or exits. Any dashboard treating this as a "live" data source is mischaracterizing its cadence.
