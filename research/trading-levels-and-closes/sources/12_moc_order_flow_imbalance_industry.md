## Relevance
Answers central question 2 (why institutions transact at the close): NAV striking for funds/ETFs, portfolio rebalancing, and benchmarking to the official closing price are the recurring institutional motives. Useful as a plain-language explanation of mechanism, though it is a vendor blog, not a rigorous source, and should be corroborated by the academic/industry sources on rebalancing (see file 15) and NBIM's closing-auction paper (file 16).
