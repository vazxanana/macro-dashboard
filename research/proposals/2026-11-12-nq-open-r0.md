# The NQ open run's trail measures R from the trailed stop (12 Nov 2026)

A defect report and a proposed fix, for the owner (docs/owner-decisions.md item 49). Nothing in `council/trader.mjs` was
changed: the fix moves where the runner trail puts its stop, which is an exit decision, so it waits for your word (absolute
rule 3). Nothing of ICT is touched. Proof: `tests/nq-open-r0.test.mjs` (offline; it lifts the loop from the trader's own text).

## In plain words

1. **What is wrong.** The NQ New York open run (records `nq-nyflip-<day>` and the regime shadow `nq-nyflipt-<day>` in
   research.json) has a runner: from +2R the stop trails 1R behind the best price (user rule `nq-runner`, `config.nqRun =
   { atR: 2, trail: 1 }`). The loop that manages it (`council/trader.mjs`, "for (const r of openNq)", line 1998 today)
   works out "1R" on every Trader run as the distance from the entry to the stop **as the stop is now**:
   `r0 = Math.abs(r.entry - r.stop)`. The records carry no opening risk (`r0` / `stop0`). Once the trail has moved the
   stop, that distance is no longer the opening risk, and three things go wrong from the next run on:
   - **the R booked at the exit**: a trailing-stop exit filled at the stop books `(stop - entry) / |entry - stop|` =
     **exactly +1.00R whatever the profit was** (less after a gap); a time exit (11:00 ET) is divided by the larger
     distance and booked too small;
   - **mfe** (best excursion, in R) is measured in the trailed unit and stops growing;
   - **the trail itself**: the new stop is `entry + (mfe - 1) x r0` with the wrong `r0`, so it jumps further than the rule
     says on every run and can be put next to, or past, the price: the trade is stopped out early.
   The result also depends on how often the Trader runs: the same candles read in one run trail correctly (the test
   shows it); read one candle per run (the quick pass runs every minute, the live runner every 30 s) they do not.
2. **The claim is confirmed, and it is larger than reported**: not only the trail exit's R and mfe but the trail's stop
   placement (an exit decision) is wrong once the stop has moved.
3. **Effect so far: none.** In the whole history of `research.json` on council-data (every commit, searched with
   `git log -S`) only two records ever carried the runner (`nq-nyflip-2026-11-09` and its regime copy
   `nq-nyflipt-2026-11-09`, both stopped out at −1.05R before reaching +2R) and **no record ever had `trailMoved`**. So
   **0 closed records are affected, 0R**; the decay monitor, the scoreboard row, the regime META tag and the digest read
   nothing wrong today. The other `nq-open` sessions have no trail (their stop never moves, so `|entry - stop|` is their
   opening risk) and are not affected.
4. **What is at stake.** The rule's whole measured edge sits in the trades this defect touches. The system replay of the
   rule (`research/replay-2w.json`, 299 `nyflip` rows, 27 Dec 2022 to 3 Nov 2026, +99.1R; made by another program on the
   futures file, which keeps the opening stop and is NOT affected) has 85 trailing-stop exits worth +165.4R (+1.95R each)
   and 65 time exits worth +84.6R; the 149 stop-outs lose −150.9R. The forward loop as written would book each of those
   85 trail exits at about +1R or less, and stop some of them out earlier. A rough sketch, not a measurement (the forward
   loop's early exits cannot be replayed without the 1-minute candles of each run): about −80R over those four years on
   the trail exits alone, about −0.27R of the replay's +0.33R a trade. A forward record kept this way would read as a
   decaying edge while the rule is unchanged.

## The proof (tests/nq-open-r0.test.mjs)

A buy at 100, stop 90 (1R = 10 points), the live push's levels (2R mark `tp1` 120, target 10R 200, trail [2, 1], out at
11:00 ET), Monday 16 Nov 2026 from 10:00 ET, one-minute candles, one Trader run per new candle:

| | Current loop | With the opening risk |
|---|---|---|
| Price reaches +2.4R (124) | stop 114 | stop 114 |
| Next run, no new high | `r0` = 14, stop jumps to 119.6 | stop 114 |
| A dip to 119 | stopped at 119.6, **booked +1.00R** (the exit is +1.96R) | still open (stop 114) |
| Instead a new high at 130 (+3.0R) | mfe stays 2.4, stop 127.44; next candle (low 127) stops it, **+1.00R** (+2.74R) | mfe 3.0, stop 120, still open |
| Instead the 11:00 ET open at 125 | **booked +1.28R** | booked +2.50R |
| A sell, mirrored | stop 86 → 80.4, gap fill at 81, **+0.97R** (+1.90R) | stop 86, still open |
| Same candles in ONE run | stop 114, open | stop 114, open |

Before the stop has moved (a stop-out at the opening stop, a target, a time exit) both loops book the same R, stop and
time (tested).

## Where the lines are

The defective line is in the management loop (`council/trader.mjs` line 1998 on 12 Nov), **outside** the sha256-pinned
block: the pin in `tests/nq-regime.test.mjs` (and the marking test in `tests/supported-book.test.mjs`) covers the push
from `      if (g && ndoOk) {` to `out at 11:00 ET.\` });` (lines 2065-2071), which this fix does not touch. No pin
test fails with the fix applied. It is still not "pure accounting": the same `r0` places the trail stop, so the fix
changes exits. Hence a proposal.

## The proposed diff

```diff
     for (const r of openNq) {
-      const s = r.side === 'sell', r0 = Math.abs(r.entry - r.stop);
+      // the OPENING risk (12 Nov 2026, item 49): the runner trail moves r.stop, so |entry - stop| is no longer 1R once it has moved
+      if (!(r.r0 > 0)) r.r0 = r.trailMoved && r.tp1 != null ? Math.abs(r.tp1 - r.entry) / 2 : Math.abs(r.entry - r.stop);
+      const s = r.side === 'sell', r0 = r.r0;
       for (const b of bars) {
```

- The loop stamps the opening risk on a record the first time it sees it open (always before the trail can move: the
  stop only moves inside this loop), as a NEW field `r0`. A record that has already trailed without `r0` (none exist)
  takes it from the push's 2R mark `tp1` (`tp1 = entry ± 2 x r0` in the pinned push), never from the trailed stop.
- Every use of `r0` in the loop (time exit, stop / trail exit, target, mfe, the trail's new stop) then reads the opening
  risk: R and mfe in the opening unit, the trail at `entry ± (mfe − 1) x opening risk`, as the rule of 5 Nov says.
- Rule 1 holds: a stop is still only ever moved toward the price (`if (s ? ns < r.stop : ns > r.stop)` is unchanged);
  no existing stop is loosened. Compared with today's loop the trail is LESS tight, because today's is wrong.
- The test file already runs the loop with exactly these three lines (`NEW` in `tests/nq-open-r0.test.mjs`); applying
  the diff means replacing the line in the trader and pointing the test's "proposed" run at the trader itself.

## What stored records would change

- **Closed records: none.** No closed record was ever booked after a trail move (above), and the fix never rewrites a
  closed record (`R`, `Rnet`, `costR` are stamped once at the close).
- **Open records:** gain the field `r0` on the next run. An open record that had already trailed (none today) would
  keep its stop (never loosened) and from then on trail and book in the opening unit.
- `research/replay-2w.json`, the decay baselines and `council/expect-base.json` are not touched (the replay keeps the
  opening stop already).

## Options

- (a) Apply the diff (recommended): the forward record of the open run then measures the rule the replay measured.
- (b) Book R and mfe from the opening risk but keep today's trail placement: the R would be right for the wrong exits,
  and the forward record would still not be the rule that was tested. Not recommended.
- (c) Leave it: harmless only while no trade reaches +2R; the first one that does is booked about +1R and may be cut early.
