# Two breakout streams as one paper book (20 Nov 2026)

Accounting for the owner, not a study: no rule is tested and there is no verdict. It puts side by side the only two streams
that passed every check so far, from their forward studies' committed baselines (`2026-11-20-two-breakouts.mjs` →
`.json`): gold's T1 breakout at the demo's measured cost (C1) and USD/JPY's T1 at the demo's cost, 1R a trade each.
**These are in-sample years for both rules.** They show what a book of the two looked like, not what it will do.

| Jan 2021 – Oct 2026 (70 months) | Trades | Total R | R a year | Month t | Worst month | Worst drawdown |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Gold T1 | 1,114 | +113.9 | +19.5 | 2.70 | −8.3 | −28.3 |
| USD/JPY T1 | 1,113 | +95.0 | +16.3 | 2.28 | −7.7 | −33.8 |
| Both | 2,227 | +209.0 | +35.8 | 3.47 | −12.1 | −42.9 |

The two streams' monthly results are almost unrelated (correlation 0.03), so the book's month t (3.47) is higher than either
alone and its return per unit of worst drawdown is better (0.83 R a year per R of drawdown, against 0.69 and 0.48). At
0.25 % a trade that is about +9 % a year with an 11 % drawdown, before any slippage beyond what each study charged.

What it does not change: both forward records started this month (gold 13 Nov, USD/JPY 20 Nov) and are the judge; item 63
(a USD/JPY demo stream) stays the owner's decision, with "wait for the forward record" recommended. Nothing live changed.
