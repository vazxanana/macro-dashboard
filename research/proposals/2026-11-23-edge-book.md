# The rules that passed, graded, and what they look like as one paper book (23 Nov 2026)

Accounting for the owner, not a study: no rule is tested and there is no verdict. Paper and demo only; nothing live changes.
Every figure below is quoted from a committed study (`research/edge/<topic>/RESULT.md` or `result.json`) or from council-data
`research/forward/*.json`; the book figures come from `2026-11-23-edge-book.mjs` → `.json` (deterministic, no network).

## The answer first

**Not five.** The research ledger (council-data `research/ledger.json`, 22 Nov) has 19 pass rows out of 533 studies. They are
**11 distinct rules**; the rest are checks of the same rule (a second feed, other years, broker ticks).

| Grade | Rules |
|---|---|
| A. Passed on years or markets it never saw, a second feed, and filled at the demo broker's own ticks | **2**: gold volatility breakout T1, USD/JPY volatility breakout T1 |
| B. Replicated on other markets, cost measured, but missed t 2 at the broker's quotes; one replication failed | **1**: gotobi fade T2 |
| C. Passed once; its replications read the same years; cost assumed | **1**: gold before a US holiday T1 |
| D. Passed once, then its replication failed or it is not robust | **4**: Turnaround Tuesday R2, volatility-spike rebound, the playbook long on silver, ICT dip-buy |
| E. Passed once, thin, never replicated | **3**: soybean short (July-September), ICT "no Friday" filter, ICT V3 htffvg |

So the honest candidate book is **four rules at most, and only two of them carry real weight**: the gold and USD/JPY breakouts.
Gotobi is close; the pre-holiday long is too rare and too weak recently to matter. None of the four is proven: the windows below
are mostly years the rules were found on, and each one's forward record is the judge.

## What the owner would decide (forward and demo records only; nothing live)

1. **Keep the records that run, and judge by them.** The gold breakout's research forward record reaches its first checkpoint at
   120 trades (about July 2027); USD/JPY's started 20 Nov; the pre-holiday's first event is Thanksgiving. The demo jobs for the two
   breakouts (`vb/trades.json`) and gotobi (`gotobi/trades.json`) measure fills and stop slippage.
2. **Gotobi has no research forward record**, only the demo job, which missed its first morning. A pre-registered
   `gotobi-forward` like the other four would be the cheap next step if the owner wants it in the book.
3. **Do not count Turnaround Tuesday, the volatility-spike rebound, the silver long or the ICT dip-buy** toward the five: their
   replications failed. The soybean short and the two ICT filters passed once on thin or failed ground.

What is missing, per rule: gold breakout, nothing but forward time; USD/JPY breakout, forward time (the ten best trades carry
75 % of the exec result, and buys carry most of it); gotobi, a forward record and t 2 at measured quotes; pre-holiday, a measured
cost and an independent replication (only the forward record can give one: no unused years are left).

Reaching five honest rules needs new rules that pass on untouched data, not more cuts of these: the overnight studies of 23 Nov
(`2026-11-23-overnight-edges.md`) added 0 of 17.
