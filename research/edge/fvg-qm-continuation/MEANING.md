# What this result means (25 Nov 2026)

The idea tested: gold falls (a continuation break), price comes back and taps a fair value gap on H1, then either an H1 QM
(entry A) or an M15 CHoCH (entry B) confirms the turn, and you sell with the target at the low of the move (buys mirror).

- **Entry A (H1 QM) almost never happens.** 81 trades in 15 years across five markets, 18 of them since 2024. Too few to
  judge, and the ones there are lose (-0.29R a trade after costs, n 18). With a QM required, the setup is rare, not good.
- **Entry B (M15 CHoCH) happens plenty and loses.** 353 trades since 2024, -0.146R a trade after costs (month t -1.74); before
  costs about -0.11R. It does not beat zero on 2010-2019 either (-0.083R, n 1,265, month t -2.08).
- Gold alone with entry B is +0.095R since 2024 (n 72, t 0.45), but -0.178R on 2010-2019 (n 262): noise, picked after looking, not a result.

So this exact chain is not an edge as written. It is one more reading that sits at zero before costs. Do not test variants on
these years (FVG size, tap rule, other timeframe); a different question needs new data and its own pre-registration.
