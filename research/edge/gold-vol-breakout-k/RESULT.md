## Data and method

- OANDA mid XAU/USD M5 through the keyless candle proxy; 5206 UTC weekdays built 2006-11-01 → 2026-11-12, 5190 New York trading days
- Rule: O = open of the first M5 candle 00:00-00:55 UTC; prevRange = high − low of the previous weekend-folded UTC D1; buy stop O + k × prevRange and sell stop O − k × prevRange on the candles up to the exit, first filled wins; stop back at O; out at the close of the candle ending at the exit. Tested rule: k 0.5, exit 21:00 UTC
- Costs: $0.60 a round trip per ounce, 2x reported; financing `FIN.xau.long` × `nights` on buys held over 22:00 UTC.
- To repeat: `node research/edge/gold-vol-breakout-k/run.mjs <dir>` with `<dir>/xau-5m.json.gz` (OANDA mid M5 XAU/USD 2006-11 on, from the candle proxy; out of git).
