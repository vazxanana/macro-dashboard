## Reported quotes/paraphrases (not independently verified against primary-source text)
> "A 1 percentage point innovation in the expected ten-year real interest rate ... is associated with a 3.4% reduction in real gold prices."

> "An extra percentage point of ten-year expected inflation raises the real gold price by a hefty 37%" — described as "well in line with the long-held 'inflation hedge' view."

> The paper organizes its empirical results around three claims: gold is a hedge against inflation, gold is sensitive to expected long-term real interest rates, and gold is regarded as protection against "bad economic times."

## Relevance
This is a central-bank-published quantification of the real-yield/gold relationship (a 100bp real-rate shock ↔ ~3.4% real gold price move), giving an actual effect size rather than just a correlation coefficient. However, note the framing is in terms of "innovations" to *expected* long-run real rates — a structural/contemporaneous response coefficient, not a demonstrated forward-predictive (out-of-sample, next-day) relationship. It also predates and is silent on the 2022-2024 period when this relationship visibly broke down (source 05), which limits how much weight this specific coefficient should carry for a live daily bias engine today.
