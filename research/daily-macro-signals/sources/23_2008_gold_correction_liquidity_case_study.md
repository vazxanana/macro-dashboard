## Relevance
A second, independent account of the same 2008 mechanism as source 22: gold was sold "indiscriminately" specifically *because* it was liquid and had performed well (i.e., it was one of the few assets institutions could actually sell at a reasonable price to raise dollars for margin calls), not because its safe-haven thesis was invalidated. This corroborates that the 2008 failure and the March 2020 failure (source 22) share the same causal structure — a systemic dollar/liquidity scramble overriding the safe-haven bid — and that gold's recovery to new highs within roughly 6 months in both cases suggests the failure is a short, acute-phase phenomenon rather than a permanent breakdown of the hedge/safe-haven property.
