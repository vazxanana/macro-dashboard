## Relevance
This is a direct, recent, peer-reviewed test of out-of-sample gold-return predictability from a broad macro variable set, and it explicitly reports the predictive horizon: two- and three-month-ahead forecasts. Even at that comparatively generous (monthly-plus) horizon, only a small subset of macro variables (inflation) showed out-of-sample predictive value, and a prominent uncertainty index (GEPU) showed none. This is strong evidence of a horizon mismatch risk: published "predictability" findings for gold operate at monthly-or-longer horizons, and the paper gives no basis to assume any of this transfers down to daily forecasting — if anything, the sparse and fragile monthly-horizon significance implied here suggests daily predictability from macro variables alone would likely be weaker still.
