## Reported quotes/paraphrases (not independently verified against primary-source text)
> A subsequent update to the original Golden Dilemma analysis suggests "the real return of gold over the next 10 years could be about -4% per year if the real price of gold mean reverts or -12% per year if the real price of gold overshoots and declines to previous low real price levels."

> The paper is framed as revisiting whether the original mean-reversion/valuation "dilemma" identified in 2013 (gold's real price being high relative to history, implying below-average subsequent real returns) still holds after gold's rally into the 2020s.

## Relevance
This is the most recent (2024) academic re-examination of the same real-yield/gold framework from source 01, by the same authors, done in light of the post-2022 environment where gold rose alongside rising/high real yields. It reinforces that the authors' own methodology operates over multi-year valuation horizons (10-year forward real returns), not daily prediction — even in this update, there is no claim of daily or short-horizon forecasting power. This is useful as corroboration that the academic literature underpinning the "real yields drive gold" narrative has never actually been tested or validated at a daily horizon by its own originators.
