## Relevance
This is the most directly relevant source found for the daily/intraday horizon specifically: it shows gold price jumps cluster tightly around scheduled macro news releases (i.e., contemporaneous, same-instant reaction to the surprise component of a release), and that elevated volatility/illiquidity actually precedes the jump by 10-15 minutes (consistent with informed positioning ahead of a scheduled release, not with the news being "predictable" from prior public real-yield levels). Critically, only 18-28% of intraday jumps are explained by scheduled news at all — meaning most jumps have other/unidentified causes. This supports the "contemporaneous, not predictive" side: the market reacts to the surprise/unexpected component of macro data at the moment of release, which by construction cannot be forecast in advance from already-public real-yield/breakeven levels.
