              How the setup works
              1. BO (breakout): on H4, a candle closes beyond the last swing low (BO Sell) or swing high (BO Buy). That sets the direction. D1 shows whether the bigger picture agrees.
              2. Clean breakout: every BO needs a candle body to close beyond the wick on the left. A wick through the level is not a break.
              3. Origin: the zone from the BO line to the high (sell) or low (buy) where the breaking move started. Price usually comes back here.
              4. VR: after the break, the move runs out and reverses on H1. That reversal area is the VR, and it is the first target.
              5. 50% line: measure the whole move after the BO, from the Origin edge where it started to the VR extreme. The halfway point (like a Fibonacci 50%) is drawn as a dashed line. A pullback that stays under it is internal structure; once price goes past it, it's the main structure.
              6. CF: once price is back inside the Origin, wait for the lower timeframe to break in the trade's direction. That break is the CF.
              7. Entry and stop: enter on the pullback to the CF level. The stop goes just past the swing the CF break started from, padded by a small buffer sized to that timeframe's candles, and never closer than 0.6 × its average candle range (ATR) or 5 × the typical spread, so normal noise can't stop it out. A body close beyond the Origin cancels the idea.
              8. Retracement trades: once the HTF has made its VR, price pulls back toward the Origin. While that happens you can trade the lower timeframe the other way: in a sell, an H1 BO Buy is a buy up to the 50% line and the Origin, and vice versa. Take profit before the Origin, because that's where the main trend resumes. These cards are tagged Retracement.
              9. Self-evolving council: every paper trade records its conditions (D1 agreeing or not, main or internal structure, session). The Coach learns rules from what loses, such as "Swing: only with D1", and keeps following blocked setups as shadow trades. If the blocked trades start winning, it drops the rule. Every rule is re-tested after 21 days. It also tracks how far each trade went for and against before closing, and turns on a break-even stop at +1R when many losers had been well in profit first.
              10. Timeframe pairs: Position D1 → H1, Swing H4 → H1, Intraday H1 → M5, Scalp M15 → M1.
