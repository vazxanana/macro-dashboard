            NQ New York open run · our system (paper)
            How it works

            NQ runs at least 0.08% of the 09:30 ET opening price (≈20 points; was a fixed 15 until 5 Nov) through it and closes back across it within 15 min; trade with the sweep (the reclaim tends to fail at the New York open), only when the midnight (00:00 ET) open lies ahead of the trade. Stop the same distance on the other side; since 5 Nov evening, after +2R the stop trails 1R behind the best price (cap 10R) instead of a fixed 2R exit (8-year test: +21.1R vs +7.4R in 2018–22, +93.7R vs +76.9R in 2023–26, drawdown not worse); out at 11:00 ET.

            Checked 4 Nov: on 8 years of Nasdaq cash (2018–2026) the same rule made +0.12R a trade over 704 trades, but 2018–2022 was about flat, so expect +0.1–0.2R a trade rather than the replay's +0.30R. Tested and not adopted: later exits, trailing stops, half off at 1R, a 10:00 ET window, volatility-scaled depth, a news-day filter, and the same rule on the S&P 500 (no edge).

              Gold replica · trades only when gold is volatile
              How it works

              Volatility gate since 4 Nov. On 8 years of spot gold (HistData 2019–2026) the replica loses in calm gold every year and only pays when gold moves a lot. Since 5 Nov it trades live only while gold's 20-day average daily range is in the top fifth of gold's own last year (judged on 2023 onwards: trades between 1.5% and that level made +0.02R a trade over 168, top-fifth trades +0.40R in both halves). Below that, every signal is still recorded here as research. It takes trades only in the daily trend direction (a pullback against the 4-hour trend); the engulfing-barrier exception was turned off on 5 Nov because it lost in every year 2023–26 (−15R over 82 trades). Replay 2023–Oct 2026 with the top-fifth gate, breakeven, the add at +2R and the runner: 212 positions, +185.9R (2024 +76.0R, 2025 +52.4R, 2026 +59.6R), worst drawdown −17.6R, best position +14.6R; the fixed-4R rules it replaces made +93.7R with a −11.7R drawdown (return per unit of drawdown 10.6 vs 7.9). Tighter stops, partial exits, session filters and stop buffers were tested on 5 Nov and rejected. Exit and scaling (since 5 Nov evening, judged on 2023 onwards): at +2R the stop moves to breakeven and a second unit is added at that level with its stop at the first entry (it shows as a separate “Replica add” trade, counted as 2R of risk); after +4R both units trail 1R behind the best price (cap 10R); out after 48 h. NQ stays the main system.

              Second gold system (paper, since 4 Nov): Mosha BO system. Weekly structure (his “Father”) sets the side; then H4 BO that way, H1 BO the other way (the retracement), M15 BO back in the H4 direction; pullback limit at the broken M15 level, stop past the M15 Origin, target 3R, valid 24 h. On 8 years of spot gold, with the weekly read the way the Trader reads it live: 2019–22 +32.2R over 87 trades, 2023–26 +34.2R over 99 (about +0.35R a trade, 7 of 8 years positive); the same setups against the weekly lost 44R. Out of sample it failed: the identical rule lost on silver, EUR/USD, GBP/USD, USD/JPY, Nasdaq and the S&P (about −220R over ~1,430 trades, 2019–26), so the gold result is probably luck in one cell of a large grid. It stays on paper as a forward test, not as a proven edge. Its trades show in Active trades and the calendar as “Mosha BO system”. Judged on 2023 onwards (5 Nov): +0.27R a trade over 101; buys against a bearish weekly when the daily trend or M15 structure agreed were tested and lost, so it stays weekly-only.

              A leg of 2+ M15 ATRs in 3 h or 6 h, then an M5 CHoCH; taken when clear of live H1 Origins or with a ★ engulfing at the extreme, with a stop of at least one M15 ATR, and (v5, 4 Nov) only against the H4 trend. Market entry, stop past the extreme, 2R, out after 48 h. The M5 gold chart marks every signal of the last 24 h.

                Pairs · crypto trend (live paper) · forex research only
                How it works

                Crypto (BTC, ETH, SOL, XRP), since 5 Nov: daily trend, two slots per coin. Buy when a daily candle closes above the 55-day high, and separately above the 100-day high. Since 5 Nov (v3) a trade exits on a daily close under the midpoint of its own 55- or 100-day channel, a level that only moves up; the 5 ATR(20) stop stays as a catastrophe stop (Sharpe 2018–21 1.67 vs 1.50, 2022–26 0.81 vs 0.58; worst drawdown −28% vs −39%). Replay 2023–Oct 2026: 65 trades, +61.7R. Long only. Picked on 2018–21 Binance history, checked on 2022–26 with 0.8% round-trip costs: +0.52R a trade over 10 coins (186 trades); on these four +52.3R over 70 trades (+0.75R), worst drawdown −7.9R. A coin stops at −8R forward.

                                The model and the grade
                                1. Sweep: price takes a confirmed H1 swing low (buys) or high (sells) from the last 4 days.
                                2. Inversion: within 6 H1 candles a candle body closes back through the last fair value gap of that move.
                                3. Entry at the price when the tracker sees it (checks every 15 min), stop 2 ticks beyond the sweep; no fixed target: from +2R the stop trails 1.5R behind the best price (since 6 Nov; signals before used a 3R target); on the index futures the stop moves to the entry at +1.5R (since 7 Nov); a fixed-2R result is recorded as a second variant; out after 36 trading hours (since 7 Nov; 48 before).
                                4. Grade /10 (candle closure rating): candle strength /4 (body and close location), inversion speed /3, room to the next liquidity /3. A+ 10 · A 9 · A- 8 · B+ 7 · B 6 · B- 5 · C 3-4 · F.
                                5. Recorded, not filtered: daily bias (vs the 50-day average), premium / discount of the 20-day range, previous-day high/low sweep, session (Asia, London, NY AM, NY PM), internal high/low validity. "What works" shows which of them matter.
                                6. Stop floor (7 Nov, part of A-setups): the stop must be at least 0.3x the daily ATR. Tight stops on H1 sweeps get hunted. Over 2023-26 on NQ, S&P and Dow it turned every year positive (+20R, +26R, +1R, +17R) and roughly halved the worst drawdown; on NQ futures 2023-25 more profit and less drawdown in every year.
                                7. Stop band (7 Nov): the stop must also be under 0.75x the daily ATR. A stop wider than that is a late, stretched entry. On 2023-26 cash data, more profit and less drawdown in both halves (+37.7R / +48.6R, drawdown 7.6 / 10.8 vs 10.1 / 11.7). NQ futures: lower drawdown in every year.
                                8. Jobs-report mornings (7 Nov): signals on the first Friday of the month before 10:00 New York are not A-setups. The 08:30 release candle sweeps on news. More profit and no more drawdown in both halves; FOMC days stay in (they held big winners).
                                9. M5 entry for Fred (7 Nov): on an index A-setup, wait up to 6 hours for a 5-minute iFVG on the same side and enter on that candle's close with the hourly stop. On 2023-26 cash data and NQ futures 2023-25 it beat the hourly entry in every year with smaller drawdowns, but only when entered on the close: 5 minutes late already loses the edge, so the tracker (every 15 min) cannot automate it.
                                10. Size by room (7 Nov): index A-setups are taken at 1.5x when the draw on liquidity is at least 2R away and 0.5x when it is closer (shown as "1.5× size" / "0.5× size"). More profit on all four test sets and a smaller drawdown on three.
                                11. Gold (7 Nov): gold futures (GC) are tracked too, with their own A-setup rule because the index rule does not carry over: buys only, stop 0.3-0.75x the daily ATR (spot gold, positive every year 2019-2026). No A+ tier. EUR/USD and GBP/USD were removed on 7 Nov, and grade F signals are no longer recorded or shown.
                                12. Tight stops at half size (7 Nov): a stop of 0.15-0.3x the daily ATR is still an A-setup when the inversion candle is strong (body 60%+) and it is not the New York afternoon, but at half size (marked ½ size). Positive in all four test sets; brings back most of this year's tight-stop winners the floor had removed.
                                13. A+: an A-setup whose inversion candle has a strong body (at least 60% of its range) and that does not fire in the NY afternoon (12-16 ET). Research 6 Nov with the trailing exit: NQ 2023 +17.1R vs +9.5R for all A-setups, 2024 +12.2R vs +9.6R, 2025 +3.1R vs +0.6R, 2026 replay +66.1R vs +67.3R with fewer trades, and a smaller worst drawdown in all four.
                                14. One per cluster: the four indices often give the same setup within hours, which is one bet, not four. This view keeps only the highest-grade index of each cluster (★). 2026 replay: 154 A-setups in 120 clusters; one per cluster +53R with a 8.9R worst drawdown vs every trade +67R with 12.9R.
                                15. A-setups (the desk's default view): the signal goes against the daily bias (prior close vs its 50-day average) and grades B+ or better (≥ 7/10). Research 6 Nov: NQ 2023-24 +0.20R a signal, NQ 2025 +0.16R, 2026 replay on all four indices +0.23R, against +0.02-0.07R for all signals; the same grade WITH the bias loses. Every signal is still recorded; switch to "All signals" to see them.
                                16. Reality check (6 Nov): on S&P 500 and Dow cash-index data 2023-26 the edge was positive in 2023-24 but flat to negative in 2025 (A+ −23R over the three indices) and depends on the data feed in 2026. It is not proven: paper only, and judge it on the live futures signals on this desk.
                                17. Backtest, NQ futures 2023-25: about +0.08R a signal, every year positive, mostly from buys. Gold liked bias + discount; NQ did not. Data from Yahoo, delayed about 10 minutes.

                                      honest versions, entries after the candle closes

                                      Rules that override everything

                                      1. Closed candles only. A level, break, rejection or engulf exists once its candle has closed.
                                      2. Wait for the Daily close. Entering before the Daily rejection had closed lost badly (PF 0.24 on days that did not close as rejections).
                                      3. Bodies break, wicks don't. Breaks, failed zones and cuts are body-close events.
                                      4. One model at a time. Run it to the end before switching.
                                      5. Structural stops. Beyond the rejection or pullback extreme, sized from that distance; fixed 20–30 pip stops don't fit today's gold.
                                      6. Regime matters. Breakouts pay in trends and lose in ranges; skip a choppy Daily.
                                      7. Levels alone are not an edge. The edge is selection + direction + trigger + management, proven in a journal.

                                      Tweaks tested (7 Nov, spot gold 2018-26)

                                      Bar to change the rule: more profit and a smaller drawdown in both 2023-24 and 2025-26. Nothing passed, so the rule stays as the skill wrote it. 2018-20, before the skill's test, also made money (+31.7R, PF 1.43).

                                      Prop sizing: the worst run was about 14R down, so 0.5% a trade at most (≈7% drawdown) and 0.25% in challenge phases. Three losses in a row = stop for the day. The edge only buys: when the Weekly turns bearish it simply stops firing.
