How the setup works
  1. BO (breakout): on H4, a candle closes beyond the last swing low (BO Sell) or swing high (BO Buy). That sets the direction.
  2. Origin: the zone from the BO line to the high (sell) or low (buy) where the breaking move started. Price usually comes back here.
  3. VR: after the break, the move runs out and reverses on H1. That reversal area is the VR, and it is the first target.
  4. CF: when price is back inside the Origin, wait for H1 to break again in the H4 direction. That confirms the trade.
  5. Entry: take it on M15 or M1 with a Barrier pattern (Simple CF or Janda). Stop goes beyond the Origin; a close beyond it cancels the idea.
