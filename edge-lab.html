Edge Lab

Every rule change we test, and whether it held up on data it never saw. Pass means the edge survived out of sample.

How to read the numbers

Expectancy is in R per trade (R = the amount risked). Each rule is fitted in sample (IS) and then checked out of sample (OOS); only the OOS number counts. "Best candidate" is the strongest rule a study tried, "vs baseline" its gain over the current system, n the number of OOS trades, max DD the deepest OOS drawdown, the bars the expectancy year by year, and the small IS / OOS bars the best candidate's expectancy in and out of sample (the dark tick is the baseline's OOS). t is the OOS t-statistic: below 2 the result could be noise, and many variants tried raise the bar. Where a study lists its trades, a line shows its cumulative R.
