## 1. MeridianX Academy (finished earlier)

An owner-only page, `/academy`, on top of the seven live Meridian systems.

- **The learning loop works on real candles.** Pick an edge → replay → the apprentice says BUY, SELL or WAIT with its
  evidence → you correct it → a lesson is saved → the same trade is replayed → the attempts are compared → the lesson is
  tested on setups it has never seen.
- **Every section reads stored data:** Train, Session, Blind arena, Progress and mistakes, Lessons, Research, Models,
  Costs, Audit, and a "What if" card.
- **Seven edges:** 2,775 setups generated by each study's own code. Each edge reproduces its study's published trade count
  and net R per trade exactly.
- **Checks at that point:** 914 tests passing, the browser audit 225 of 225, CI green.

Honest results from that stage:

- The decision policy does **not** beat simply taking every setup (1,421 training trades: it takes +0.342R, skips +0.201R,
  difference t 1.17, not significant).
- No trained scorer beats taking every setup. All are stored as rejected; none is promoted.
- The research engine validated nothing (420 conditions registered, 121 testable, 0 survive the correction for many tests).

## 3. Today's three requests

### The mirror was not taking replica trades: fixed, merged

- **Cause.** The mirror read only the council's main trade list and the ICT list. The replica trades are recorded in the
  research file, which it never read. The Replica demo account sat empty at 10,000 since 8 Nov.
- **Fix.** The mirror now adds those trades to the replica book, through the same path as every other trade: a stop is
  required, the same limits apply, stop moves and closes follow the record.
- **Guards.** A trade also in the main list is never copied twice. A trade whose own filter failed counts as a shadow.
  They are copied only while the replica book has its own demo account, so they cannot land on the council account.
- **State.** Merged to `main` as PR #709 after test, build and page checks all passed. About 30 demo positions should
  open in the Replica account on the mirror's next rounds. A check is scheduled to confirm it.
