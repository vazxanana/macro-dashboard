Candles are OANDA mid prices; the future is hidden. Buy or Sell puts a trade on the chart: drag its entry, stop and target lines (red = risk, green = reward), then Confirm. Open trades' stop and target can be dragged too. Orders fill on the next bars; when a stop and a target sit inside one bar the stop is assumed first unless you switch it off in Account.
