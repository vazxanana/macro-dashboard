Market Structure
External — Bullish (HH / HL → BO Buy)
Price forms Higher High → Higher Low, then breaks above prev HH = BO Buy ↑
External — Bearish (LH / LL → BO Sell)
Price forms Lower High → Lower Low, then breaks below prev LL = BO Sell ↓
Internal — i-Breakout Sell
After E.High forms, i-BO Sell occurs within the HTF pullback — early bearish signal.
Internal — i-Breakout Buy
After E.Low forms, i-BO Buy occurs within the HTF pullback — early bullish signal.
Entry Model 1 — Breakout System (BO → VR → CF)
Buy Setup
H4 BO Buy → rally tops out in the H1 VR → pullback into the Origin forms the H1 CF → Entry ↑
Sell Setup
H4 BO Sell → drop bottoms out in the H1 VR → pullback into the Origin forms the H1 CF → Entry ↓

Case 3: CF breaks VR ⚠
HTF sweep / manipulation. Risky — repeat SOP.
Case 4: Sideways
Price cannot break VR or CF. Barrier Entry only.
