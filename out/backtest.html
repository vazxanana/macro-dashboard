How it works
  • Replay. Real OANDA candles. Everything after your start stays hidden until you play it: Play / pause Space, one bar →, undo a bar ←, skip to the next day, or jump to a date J. Speed 1× to 12×.
  • Trade in one click. Buy / Sell (B / S) opens the trade at the price now. The stop is placed for you (1 ATR, or the last stop preset you picked), the target at 2R, and the size comes from your risk %: if the stop is hit you lose that share of the account. Every trade has a stop.
  • Or set it up first. Shift+B / S, Shift+drag from the entry to the stop, or "Set up an order…" puts a draft on the chart: drag its lines (red = risk, green = reward), pick 1R / 2R / 3R (keys 1 2 3), then Confirm Enter.
  • If the price moved. A confirm is never refused: the trade goes in at the price now with the same stop distance and R:R, a stop or target on the wrong side is mirrored, and a limit the price already passed fills at market.
  • Fills. Orders fill on the next bars. When the stop and the target are both inside one bar, the stop counts first (the worst case) unless you change it in Account → Settings.
  • Up to four charts. Layout puts two, three or four charts side by side: the same market at the same moment, each on its own timeframe (1 minute to 1 week). The other charts never show a candle from the future; the last candle of a higher timeframe is still forming. Your open trades show on all of them, and you trade on the first chart.
  • Toolbar. The market and timeframe (opens the session form, where the timeframes from 1 minute to 1 day are) · Replay (undo, next, play, skip day, jump, speed) · the time · Chart tools (Layers, Layout, Draw) · View (sound, full screen, toolbar position).
