## What it means, and what it does not

- **The dates matter more than they should.** Starting the same short two weeks later (mid-July to mid-October) makes it a
  loser on 2016-26 (four winning years of the published window, 2018, 2019, 2022 and 2025, lose), and a one-week
  earlier start nearly erases it.
  A real, broad pre-harvest decline would be expected to show over nearby windows too; here the 2016-26 figure leans on the
  calendar-month boundaries the study happened to fix in advance.
- **The stop is not the weak point.** 2 x and 4 x ATR(20) keep the 2016-26 mean positive (+0.634R and +0.318R).
- **n is still 11 years**, the per-year t of every variant is under 1.2 on 2016-26, and the pattern has been in print for
  decades. Nothing here is an edge.
- **What it allows.** At most a candidate paper shadow for the owner, with "FRAGILE: a 10-day later window loses on 2016-26"
  beside its n and p. Nothing live changed; no market was added to any rule.
