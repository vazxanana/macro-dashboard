## What it means

No zone quality picked better trades on the confirmation years after being chosen on the discovery years, and no cell
cleared the bar. Do not re-test variants of these splits on these years. Nothing live changed; no rule is proposed.

**The zone is not what it measures.** Buying (selling) an H4 engulf zone on an M15 close inside it on its first return made
+0.018R a trade before costs in 2015-26 (n 29,663, month t 2.61; discovery +0.023R, t 3.55), but a random band of the
same height at a matched distance, entered the same way, made +0.015R (t 2.21; Welch t of the difference 0.26). The small
tilt is the short pull-back into any level below (above) the price, not the engulf. After costs it loses
(-0.051R, t -7.18). The fade of the same moment loses before costs (-0.026R, t -4.34): the fade that
`grid7-wide-fade` found works after lower-timeframe breaks, not on a close back inside a higher-timeframe zone.
