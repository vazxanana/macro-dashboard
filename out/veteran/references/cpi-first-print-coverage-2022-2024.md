Earlier monthly columns can contain revised history and were not substituted. Raw tokens, source hashes, headers, receipt metadata and comparators are retained in the [JSON ledger](cpi-first-print-release-ledger-2022-2024.json).

The [validation record](cpi-first-print-validation-2022-2024.json) confirms 144 field, 36 date, 36 source-hash and 36 scheduled-UTC agreements; five invalid-input controls were rejected. Numerical agreement verifies extraction of the captured archive, not absence of historical corrections. Two embedded reissue notes were missed by the independent helper and are corrected in `work/bls-first-print-independent-review-addendum.md`.

The January 2022 PDF was also reissued; BLS says the text version was correct as originally published. This extraction used the archived HTML/PRE text, not that PDF. The October 2024 companion spreadsheet had an adjustment-label correction; it was not used for these figures. The official register was checked through the captured 2026 entries. No identified note changed these selected Table A fields; original-day bytes were not independently recovered. [BLS errata](https://www.bls.gov/errata/)

All release headers state 08:30 ET. Scheduled UTC uses America/New_York and seasonal daylight time; it is not a measured publication receipt. May 2024's documented early posting remains flagged, with precise field availability missing. See the [release-integrity case](bls-release-integrity-case-2026-11-09.md).
