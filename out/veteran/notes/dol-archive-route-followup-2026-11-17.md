# DOL weekly claims archive route follow-up — 17 November 2026

**Official route found.** The [OUI “Choose a Weekly Report” page](https://oui.doleta.gov/unemploy/claims_arch.asp) exposes a form with `method="post"`, `action="archive.asp"`, `report=press` (News Release), and a year selector including 2022–2024. Submitting the form for each year renders a corresponding calendar at [archive.asp](https://oui.doleta.gov/unemploy/archive.asp). This explains why GET requests with `year=2024`, `yr=2024` and `CY=2024` all returned the same 2025 page in the earlier audit: the application reads the POST selection.

I submitted the official form in the browser and extracted the PDF anchors shown in each rendered calendar. The [JSON inventory](dol-archive-route-followup-2026-11-17.json) records **52 links for 2022, 52 for 2023, and 52 for 2024**, with each displayed release date and official PDF URL. The 2022, 2023 and 2024 rendered DOM snapshots had respective SHA-256 values `c8b312ad980884a50739f71433973d064e0e339a02f611abcb799edd8a8f4389`, `e288cd2a50f4d806b2d063773c9d6ebe3ea15ca87dde05663579afa188ae98cb`, and `14542979ea859cacd6d2dfedf2b03a04fca39fa75feb5c1d0b165b51c2c60145`. These hashes describe browser DOM HTML after links loaded, **not raw HTTP response bytes or PDF contents**.

The calendars include Wednesday 2022-11-23, 2023-11-22, 2024-07-03 and 2024-11-27. The [OUI publication schedule](https://oui.doleta.gov/unemploy/claims_arch.asp) says Thursday at 8:30 a.m. Eastern with federal-holiday exceptions; that is a schedule statement, not proof of actual first availability for every historical file. The earlier [eight-PDF sample](claims-ip-overlap-route-2026-11-17.md) independently found 8:30 a.m. Eastern embargo headers on those sampled releases. The remaining 148 PDFs were not fetched or header-checked here.

This is an **official calendar-link inventory**, not an exhaustive audit of PDF existence, corrected or reissued versions, unlisted publications, or first-publication timestamps. Those checks remain open before treating DOL coverage as complete for the frozen overlap gate.
